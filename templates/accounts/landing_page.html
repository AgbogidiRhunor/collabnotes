Features

Everything your team needs

Powerful features built for seamless collaboration, security, and productivity.

Real-time Editing

Edit notes simultaneously with your team. Changes appear instantly — no refresh needed.

Author Highlights

Every edit is colour-coded by author. Hover any text to see exactly who wrote it.

Role-based Access

Control who can view, edit, or manage notes with granular Owner, Editor, and Viewer roles.

Version History

Every save creates a snapshot. Browse the full history and restore any previous version instantly.

Easy Sharing

Share via email invite or a time-limited link. Recipients get the exact access level you set.

Workspaces

Organise notes into colour-coded folders. Share entire workspaces with your team in one click.
